    Take a mod out of your modlist, cleanly

    ModTrap finds every plugin that depends on a mod you want gone, and works out what it takes to remove that dependency. At this stage it only reads your modlist: nothing is changed.

    1. Open your modlist. Drag its folder (the one with ModOrganizer.exe in it) anywhere onto this page, or paste its path above.
    2. Tick the mods you want to take out. ModTrap shows which other plugins need them.
    3. Run a dry run. Your own xEdit lists exactly what would be taken out of each of those plugins.
